Our History

1Nest Poultry Farm was founded by Ms. Selina Khaalo in the rural village of Zebediela, Limpopo. The idea behind the farm was inspired by the classic rhetorical question, "What came first, the chicken or the egg?" Believing in the power of the egg, Ms. Khaalo set out to show how much life and opportunity a single egg can create.

Recognizing a gap in the local market and a lack of agricultural businesses in her village, she acted with vision. She aimed to put her hometown on the map while providing essential job opportunities for local residents. Today, our driving mission remains focused on creating a sustainable poultry powerhouse where nothing goes to waste.
