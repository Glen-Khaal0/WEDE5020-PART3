Our Story

One woman, one village, one egg at a time

How It Started

We are inspired by the scarcity of female black-owned farms in our village and in South Africa. While bridging the gap, we aim to motivate people to do what they can with what they have at their disposal, while also uplifting their fellow people. To Ms Khaalo, the question of what came first, the chicken or the egg, was never really the point. What mattered was what came next: jobs for her neighbours, food for nearby tables, and a name for Zebediela beyond its own borders.

"It was never about the egg or the chicken. It was about what either one could become, if somebody just gave it a home."

— Ms Selina Khaalo, Founder
